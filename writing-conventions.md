# Writing conventions

- **Write all repo artifacts in English** — code, comments, commit messages, PR text,
  and technical docs.
- Keep published text professional and impersonal (no individual names).
- **Cheap review techniques for any artifact**: re-read it after 24 hours with
  fresh eyes; hand it to a reader outside the authoring team; and use "can you
  tell me what the user does?" as a clarity test — if the reader cannot answer,
  the document is not clear enough.
